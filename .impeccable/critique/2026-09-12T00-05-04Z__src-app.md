## Design Specificity Verdict

**The page bodies could not belong to another product. The shell and the interactive layer could belong to any of them.**

Where `DESIGN.md` is prescriptive, the site is unmistakably itself: the wall-label-plus-hairline row, the `/professional` tools ledger where each tool names the artifact that proves it, the masthead with the surname in Forest Ink beside a square hairline-bordered portrait plate. A second, generic site begins exactly where the document stops giving orders — overlays, modals, filters, and the mobile shell: three floating rounded pills where the doc describes one control pill; two filter languages for one job (`/projects` bordered chips with counts, `/blog` bare mono links with no active state); a stock command palette whose descriptions ("View my portfolio projects") are in a voice from another site.

Largest visual masses on `/` and `/projects` are three near-black UI screenshots and a blue-lavender gradient tile — on warm paper, in a system with one green pen. Neumorphic shadows were correctly identified as the old template tell and removed; dark screenshots tiled three-up are the new one, and they are bigger.

Structural sameness is inverted from the usual: not repetition, but inconsistency of the same thing. `/blog` groups 84 essays into five themed sections with counts and descriptions; `/projects` dumps 20 flat with no grouping. Same author, same week, same system.

Biggest missed opportunity: the site runs its own view counter and retrieval corpus, yet "Most read" shows three bare titles with no counts or dates, and home essay rows drop the date and stage that identical `/blog` rows carry. A site whose identity is "keeps records honestly" withholds its own records where it would be most distinctive.

**Deterministic scan.** `detect.mjs` over `src/app src/components`: exit 2, **15 findings** (`design-system-font-size` ×5, `design-system-radius` ×3, `design-system-color` ×3, `overused-font` ×2, `bounce-easing` ×1, `layout-transition` ×1). Verified against source: **3 real, 12 false positives** — consistent with the documented ~13/15 FP rate across three prior cycles.

Confirmed: (1) a second undocumented green — `og/route.tsx:52-53` sets `GREEN = '#135c34'` and `MUTED = '#5c574c'` where `DESIGN.md` declares `forest-ink #184e35` / `ink-muted #606976`; used ~8× and shipping on every social card. `console-greeting.tsx:26-27` carries the same rogue `#135c34`. (2) `globals.css:735` transitions `padding-inline-end` on `.site-shell`, which wraps the whole app — a layout property animated on the document wrapper; the detector under-reported it, missing `.site-header`'s `inset-inline-end` at `:740`.

## Overall Impression

The foundation is genuinely excellent and the execution is genuinely inconsistent, and the gap between those two facts is the whole report. `DESIGN.md` is the best committed design document on a personal site I have reviewed — it names rules, records the incident behind each, and admits where the doc was wrong before the code was. The deterministic surface is correspondingly clean: one `h1` per route, zero heading-order skips, zero missing `alt`, zero unnamed controls, zero horizontal overflow at 390px, a working skip link as first tab stop, and **zero contrast failures in dark mode** across all five routes (137–309 text elements per page).

The single biggest opportunity: **the rules only bind where they are written down, and the drift has migrated to where they aren't.** The three sharpest defects are invisible to the 17-rule drift suite by *scope*, not by allowance — a third navigation defined outside `constants/navlinks`, a heading that changes ramp step across a breakpoint, and a three-word Title Case heading below the rule's `words.length >= 4` threshold. The next move is probably not more rules; it is one rule about where vocabulary is allowed to be defined at all.

## What's Working

**1. The wall-label + hairline row system.** It works mechanically, not just aesthetically: because metadata has a fixed unmistakable voice (IBM Plex Mono 500, 0.72rem, 0.16em, uppercase, tabular), a title never fights a date for weight — so the row needs no card, and the hairline does the containing work a border-plus-shadow would. That is why `/blog` and `/professional` read as a catalogue rather than a feed. The tools ledger is the strongest single idea: thirty tools each naming the artifact that proves it, which requires having the artifacts.

**2. The focus system as a default rather than an opt-in.** Measured across 13 settled tab stops: uniformly `outline: 2px solid rgb(24,78,53)` at `outline-offset: 2px` — nav links, buttons, theme toggle, CTA alike. Skip link reveals correctly at `top: 16px` in Forest Ink and is the first anchor in the DOM. One treatment, in the site's own ink, applied globally with two documented exceptions.

**3. The counts are derived, not asserted.** "Eighty-four essays" matches 84 `content.mdx` files via `spell-count.ts`, guarded by `count-drift.test.ts`. (`CLAUDE.md` says 83 — the document is stale, the site is right.) On a page whose lead essay is "The Audit Only Checks What It Can Reach," numbers that check themselves are the design system agreeing with the content.

## Priority Issues

### [P1] One component renders three ways; one feature has five names
The suggested-questions component renders as wall-label links on home (the documented form), as four bordered rounded body-sans boxes in a ragged 522/610/761/550px staircase on `/chat`, and as `↳`-prefixed body-sans links in the ask drawer. `DESIGN.md` says verbatim: *"Suggested questions and tag lists are not chips at all: they are wall-label links… not pills."* The feature is called "Ask this site" (nav), "Ask the site anything" (home), "Ask the essays anything." (drawer), "Chat with Lorenzo" (`/chat` h1), "AI Chat" (palette). Home's questions navigate to `/chat?q=…`; the drawer answers in place — two implementations, five names, two behaviours, all reachable from one page.
**Why it matters:** a returning reader cannot form a model of what "ask" does or whether the drawer and `/chat` are the same conversation. The `/chat` variant is the messiest block on the site and it is the flagship interaction.
**Fix:** extract one `SuggestedQuestions` in the documented wall-label-link form, consumed by all three surfaces; pick one name; decide whether `/chat` or the drawer is the product and make the other route to it.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

### [P1] `/projects` ships a duplicate, and its own count contradicts the page
Three independent signals: `merge-gate.0g99n9zayrbtq.webp` is referenced 3×; "Decide what merges without a human" renders twice as a visible `h2`; 20 project `h2`s render while the filter chip reads `ALL 18`. The duplicate occupies positions 1 and 3 of the first preview row.
**Why it matters:** this is the first thing a hiring manager sees below the filters. A portfolio showing one project twice under a count that says 18 when 20 render undercuts the exact claim of the site's headline essay about audits that only check what they can reach.
**Fix:** de-duplicate the source (likely a featured entry re-included in the list); derive `ALL n` from the rendered collection; add a `projects-count-drift` test in the shape of the existing `count-drift.test.ts` — the machinery exists.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

### [P1] The command palette is an unowned third navigation
`use-command-palette.tsx` hardcodes nav items outside `src/constants/navlinks`. `/blog` is "Blog" there but "Writing" in both persistent navs; `/chat` is "AI Chat" vs "Chat"; `/professional` is absent although both navs promote it as "Hire me". Also absent: Lab, Now, Books, Movies, Music, Photos, Uses, Topics, Garden, Changelog, Links, Stats, Colophon — 8 of ~30 destinations. `"Switch to Dark Mode"` is Title Case. The drift suite has a rule literally named "gives every destination exactly one name", written after this exact bug, but it imports `@/constants/navlinks`, so the palette is invisible to it by scope. Separately the palette has `box-shadow: none` and no scrim, sits flush at `y=8` over the fixed nav, covers the Search button that opened it, and clips its 9th row mid-glyph.
**Why it matters:** the palette is the power user's primary surface, it renames what they learned, cannot reach the hire page, and does not read as a layer.
**Fix:** source palette items from `constants/navlinks` and broaden the vocabulary rule to scan `href`+label pairs anywhere in `src`; give the overlay a paper-tinted scrim (a scrim is not a shadow, so the Flat Paper Rule survives); rewrite or delete the eight off-voice descriptions.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

### [P1] `/contact` has no primary action, and the mobile ask field is clipped
`Book a call ↗` and `Email Directly` are both `button-default` (Paper Card fill, hairline, Ink text) with zero Forest Ink on the page, in two casing conventions, only one carrying `↗` although both leave the site; two hairlines at y≈585 and y≈628 enclose a 43px empty band. `DESIGN.md`: *"Primary… Forest Ink fill, white text… One per view."* At 390px the ask placeholder renders as "What have you changed you" — hard-clipped mid-word, colliding with the overlapping Ask button.
**Why it matters:** these are the two points of highest intent. One gives no answer to "which button do I press"; the other looks broken at the width most visitors use.
**Fix:** make `Book a call` the one `cta-primary`, demote `Email directly` to sentence-case `button-ghost`, drop the stray `↗`, close the empty band. Below `sm`, stack the ask button under the rule or make it an icon-only round submit, and shorten the placeholder.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

### [P2] The mobile shell has no identity and no way home
At 390px on every route `a[href="/"]` has `offsetParent === null`: the desktop header is `display:none` and the mobile bar carries no wordmark, leaving one unlabeled hamburger floating with no bar behind it. The site's name appears nowhere until the footer, ~7,700px down.
**Why it matters:** the dominant arrival path for a personal site is a shared link opened on a phone. That reader cannot learn whose site this is or reach home in one tap.
**Fix:** put the wordmark (or "Lorenzo Scaturchio" at the card-title step in Fraunces) in the mobile bar as a link to `/`, left of the hamburger, on a paper bar with the 40% hairline beneath — the structure the desktop bar already has.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable adapt

### [P2] `/lab` undercuts the competence it exists to prove
Result excerpts render un-processed MDX — a leading `#` hash, a bare ``` fence — in italic, clipped mid-glyph, tagged `keyword match` on a page headed "Semantic search demo"; "Toy text similarity" opens with two obviously related default sentences scoring 0%. Assessment B independently confirmed two machine-checkable defects in the same file: the result snippet measures **3.48:1 contrast** in light mode (needs 4.5; the `/80` opacity modifier is the cause — full strength measures 5.23:1), and the search input has **no programmatic label** (no `id`, no `<label>`, no `aria-label`; placeholder-only, and it disappears on typing).
**Why it matters:** `/lab` exists solely to demonstrate the author can build retrieval systems. It currently demonstrates one that returns markdown syntax and falls back to keyword matching — and it is the only page on the site with a WCAG contrast failure.
**Fix:** strip markdown from excerpts at index time in `sync-retrieval-corpus`; clamp on line boundaries; drop the `/80` modifier; add a real label; set dates in `label-mono`; replace nested boxes with the hairline-row pattern.
**Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

## Persona Red Flags

**Hiring manager, 30 seconds, `/professional` → `/projects`:** cards 1 and 3 are the identical merge-gate image, under a chip saying 18 while 20 headings render. Every `h2` is a value proposition, never a project name — ⌘F for `merge-gate` matches no heading. The proof numbers (`49 PASSING TESTS · ~70 REPOS GOVERNED · ZERO ARMS GRANTED`) are the most persuasive text on the page and are set in the least readable style the system offers, 11.52px uppercase mono at 0.16em, wrapping to three lines. On `/professional` the employer reads as a watermark: company names are Fraunces 20px in `#606976` (the *metadata* colour) while the role beneath is Instrument Sans semibold in full Ink — both rules inverted at once. The tools ledger's project names (`Talker`, `Remedi`, `unlinkd`, `merge-gate`) are plain text, not links: the one element tying a skill to its proof has no path to the proof. The ledger is also alphabetised row-major, so scanning the left column yields C, E, F, I, M, N, O, P, P, p, R, S, S, T.

**First-time reader from a shared essay link, on a phone:** cannot learn whose site this is — no wordmark, no home link, one unlabeled hamburger; footer is ~7,700px away. Passes eight strata before the first sentence, including an 8-item uppercase mono chain with an orphaned `·` at the line break and a visible `— VIEWS`. `EXPLORE PHILOSOPHY & THE SELF TECHNOLOGY & ATTENTION` has no separator between its two links, so it reads as one six-word phrase while the line above it uses `·`. The masthead's portrait caption wraps to three lines and is wider than the plate it labels.

**Keyboard-driven returning reader:** ⌘K reaches 8 of ~30 destinations and renames what they learned. The palette doesn't read as a layer — no scrim, `box-shadow: none`, covering the button that opened it. In the drawer, "start a new conversation" is an unlabeled `↺` and send is a bare muted `↑` with no chrome. The drawer's documented push-the-shell behaviour is gated above 1536px, so at 1440 the document's most carefully argued interaction never happens. And the focus ring is right at rest but wrong in motion: Tailwind v4's `transition-colors` includes `outline-color`, so the 2px Forest Ink ring fades in from the control's own text colour over 150ms — on the primary CTA that start colour is white.

## Questions to Consider

1. **Is the Wall Label Rule labelling, or hiding?** The most persuasive sentence on the home page is set in the least readable style the system offers. When does a label stop captioning the work and start being the work?
2. **When a layer genuinely has to be a layer, which rule loses?** The Flat Paper Rule is why the palette has no shadow (correct) and also why it has no scrim, and therefore why it doesn't read as a modal. The document needs a third sanctioned separation mechanism.
3. **If "New here? Start with these" exists for a first-time reader, why is it section 04 of 4, at y≈4,000 of a 5,529px page?**
4. **Two chat surfaces, five names, two behaviours — which one is the product?**
5. **The three sharpest defects are invisible to the drift suite by scope, not allowance.** Is the next move more rules, or one rule about where vocabulary may be defined at all?
6. **Was the imagery chosen for the night variant?** Every hero and thumbnail is a dark image; the default page is warm paper. In dark they look deliberate; in light they are holes.
7. **`/blog` groups 84 essays into five themed sections; `/projects` lists 20 flat.** Why did only one index get designed?
