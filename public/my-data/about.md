I'm Lorenzo Scaturchio, an AI engineer based in Los Angeles. I build VivanceData (https://www.vivancedata.com), which brings AI to construction, HVAC, logistics and manufacturing businesses: after-hours calls answered and booked, and permits and delivery paperwork read instead of re-keyed. I also take on a few consulting projects; the terms are at lscaturchio.xyz/work-with-me.

Before that I was a machine learning engineer at Sizzle, a data engineer at the Joint Genome Institute, and an intern running hydropower models on a supercomputer at VICE Lab. I have a master's in Applied Data Science from USC; my capstone was Talker, a retrieval-augmented teaching assistant.

What I build on my own time:

- Tools that keep my own work maintained. A fleet of scheduled Claude routines looks after my repositories, and merge-gate, a deterministic policy engine, decides which of their pull requests can merge without me.
- Games and communities. Monkey SMP is a modded Minecraft server for friends, and Front Desk is the Discord bot for the Chill Gamers community, bridged to that server. Hollow Crown is a rooftop stealth game I am building in Godot.
- Software that respects attention and privacy: Cocoon and Feedless (browser extensions), Verso (an offline-first art diary) and unlinkd (local-first evidence for data-broker removal).
- Self-hosting: a K3s homelab with a declarative service catalogue, after an earlier Raspberry Pi version.

I care about measuring things before believing them. My trading bot's early backtests said +646%; they were measurement bugs, the honest number is that it does not beat buying the index, and the bugs became a tool, backtest-audit, that catches them.

Outside of code I make music, indie folk with industrial textures. A five-song EP is being recorded, and it is not out yet. I also write essays on this site and keep logs of the books and films I get through.
