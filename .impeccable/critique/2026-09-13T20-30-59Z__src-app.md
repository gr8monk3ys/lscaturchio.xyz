## False positives screened out before they became deductions

A prior browser sweep produced four categories of finding, all verified false against the DOM:

- **"Overflowing elements"** — `ASIDE.ask-drawer` and nine descendants at `right=780` on a 390px viewport: a closed off-canvas drawer, `position: fixed`, contributing nothing to scroll width. The same ten elements on all 25 renders; the probe capped the list at 10.
- **"Unnamed interactive elements"** — the probe read `innerText`, which is `''` for anything not rendered. Hits were suggested-question buttons in the closed drawer and 149 book links inside six collapsed `<details>`. All carry real text.
- **"Truncated text without title"** — every hit had `clientWidth` of exactly 1px: the `sr-only` signature, intentionally clipped.
- **"Contrast failure on `SPAN.text-foreground/25`"** — a decorative `aria-hidden` middot at 1.68:1, separating elements that also carry `gap-x-3`.

Four stale claims from the 23/40 snapshot were re-checked and are fixed: footer socials carry `aria-label`, the `— views` degraded state is `invisible` + `aria-hidden`, the arrow glyph is gone from `/projects`, and the hover hint is behind `hidden can-hover:block`.
