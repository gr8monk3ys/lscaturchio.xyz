# Product

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: a reader who arrived through an essay (search, a share, the RSS feed, a
webmention) and wants to read more and see who wrote it. They are on a phone or
a laptop, mid-read, with no prior relationship to the author.

Secondary, confirmed: someone evaluating Lorenzo for work (a hiring manager or a
prospective consulting client) who lands on /professional, /work-with-me or
/services. They are served, but they never lead. Hiring and consulting surfaces
exist; the home page and navigation do not shout them.

Also a real user: the owner. The site is a personal garden the author adds to
for his own use and pleasure. (Decided 2026-06-04; reconfirmed 2026-09-01.)

## Product Purpose

lscaturchio.xyz is Lorenzo Scaturchio's personal site: 83 essays on power,
attention and institutions, a portfolio of AI and web systems, a chat box that
answers from the essays, and a growing garden of everything else he does
(films, music, books, photos, a guestbook, a now page, a roadmap, a lab).

Success is a reader who stays, reads a second essay, and leaves knowing who
the author is. A hire or a consulting enquiry is a welcome side effect, not the
measure.

## Positioning

**One person, everything, honestly.** The site holds essays, code, films,
music, books, photos and work history in one place, and every number on it is
sourced. Breadth plus verified truth is the position; a neighbouring personal
site can copy the breadth or the honesty, but rarely both.

The current hero line, "builds systems, suspicious of them", is a tagline, not
a commitment. Future work may replace it. (Confirmed 2026-09-01.)

## Operating Context

- Content is authored in the repo: essays are `src/app/blog/<slug>/content.mdx`,
  structured content lives in `src/constants/*`, and there is an admin surface
  (`/admin`) for posts, links, photos and the now page.
- Several sections are fed by automation rather than by hand: media CSVs in
  `public/my-data` arrive weekly by launchd job from Letterboxd, Goodreads and
  Spotify RSS; the chat retrieval corpus (`public/my-data/blog-*.md`) is
  generated by `bun run sync-retrieval-corpus`; blog audio is generated and
  served from Vercel Blob; project logos and Matisse-style covers are script
  generated.
- Deploys go to Vercel from `main`. CI gates every PR on lint, typecheck, unit
  tests with coverage thresholds, build, and Playwright e2e. A drift check fails
  CI if an essay changes without the corpus being re-synced.
- The chat box uses OpenAI over the essay corpus; Neon Postgres holds views,
  guestbook entries, subscriptions and similar state. One database serves
  local and production.
- Maintenance model (agreed 2026-06-04): shared infrastructure (`src/lib`, API
  contracts, the MDX substrate) gets rigor; individual sections are best-effort
  toys, and guards must stay non-blocking so adding a new kind of content is
  never taxed.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Confirmed capabilities: essays with series, tags and topics; per-essay audio;
site-wide chat over the essays; projects with case studies; professional
timeline reconciled against the resume repo; services and work-with-me pages;
media pages (movies, music, books) from exported data; photos; guestbook;
bookmarks and links; now page; changelog; roadmap; stats; colophon; uses; lab
and secret pages; newsletter subscribe and unsubscribe; contact form; RSS and
webmentions; an offline page and service worker; an API docs page.

Binding constraints (confirmed 2026-09-01):

- **No unverifiable claims, ever.** No number, outcome, testimonial, client
  name or accuracy figure may appear on the site without a source that can be
  checked. The `fix/honest-claims` work removed fabricated testimonials,
  invented outcome percentages and skills no repo supports; nothing of that
  kind may return. Where the controlled comparison was never run, the site
  says so instead of estimating.
- **Breadth is a feature.** New content types keep arriving. Never propose
  trimming sections or scope. Adding a section must stay cheap, and a section
  with no data yet renders an honest empty state (see /music) rather than
  disappearing or faking content.
- **The essay voice is the house voice.** `docs/writing-style.md` governs all
  copy, including UI labels, empty states, error messages and microcopy, not
  only posts.
- **Multilingual is real, not a toy.** Locale routing for en, es, fr, hi, ar
  and zh-cn (`src/proxy.ts`, Google Translate cookie) is a commitment. Layouts
  must survive right-to-left text and strings far longer than the English.
- Technical: Next.js 16 App Router (Server Components by default; the
  framework's docs in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` override training
  assumptions), React 19, Tailwind, Bun. Blog metadata helpers stay in
  `src/lib/blog-data.ts` and `src/lib/getAllBlogs.ts`; the sitemap is
  `src/app/sitemap.ts`.

Terminology: "essay" and "post" are both used for `/blog` entries; "garden" is
the collective name for the non-essay sections; "the corpus" is the retrieval
set the chat answers from.

Absences future work must not fill in:

- **No testimonials exist.** `src/constants/testimonials.tsx` is empty on
  purpose; the section renders nothing until real entries with genuine profile
  URLs are added.
- **No client outcome metrics.** Services copy states explicitly that no
  outcome percentage is published because the controlled comparison was never
  run.
- **No named consulting clients or logos.** Upwork work is described by count
  and kind (4 clients, RAG document Q&A, fine-tuned classification), not by
  name.
- **No press, awards or case-study numbers** beyond those in the timeline.

## Product Principles

1. **The reader leads; the hirer follows.** Every surface is designed for
   someone mid-read who wants more, and only then for someone evaluating.
2. **Every number has a source, or it is not on the page.** Honesty is a
   positioning claim, so a single fabricated figure breaks the whole product.
3. **Adding is cheap, removing is not the answer.** The garden grows; design
   for new sections to slot in with an honest empty state on day one.
4. **One voice everywhere.** The essays' voice is the interface's voice; copy
   in a button or an error is held to the same standard as a paragraph.
5. **Rot, not size, is the risk.** Prefer drift guards and generated content
   over hand-maintained surfaces that silently go stale.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No formal standard has been set. Established requirements: the site must work
in right-to-left scripts (Arabic) and with strings much longer than English
(Hindi, Spanish); the offline page and service worker must keep essays readable
on poor connections; per-essay audio exists as a listening alternative for
every post. WCAG conformance level is undecided.
